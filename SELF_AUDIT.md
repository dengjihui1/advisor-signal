# Self-audit

## Verified

- The Skill separates profile facts, live sources, inferences, assumptions, and recommendations.
- A supervisor shortlist is required before target-specific writing.
- The workflow limits selected papers to two and requires source URLs and reading depth.
- The validator catches missing sources, invalid score ranges, excess papers, missing doctoral asks, and common placeholders.
- The demo contains synthetic information only and passes the validator.
- The package does not require network access, an API key, email access, or upload of applicant files.

## Not established

- A fit score cannot predict admission or supervisor response.
- The MVP does not automatically verify every claim on the live web; source verification remains part of the agent workflow.
- It does not replace reading the selected papers, checking program rules, or human review before sending.
- No user study yet establishes better response rates than a careful manual workflow.

## Falsifiable next checks

1. Build a dated benchmark of supervisor pages and paper metadata to measure source accuracy.
2. Compare unsupported-claim rate against a generic LLM email prompt.
3. Ask applicants whether decision cards improve target selection before measuring email response rate.
